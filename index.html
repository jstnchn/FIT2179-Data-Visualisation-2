Malaysia's tourism story — who visits, where they land, and how the industry rose, crashed, and came back.

01

Who Visits Malaysia?

Which Countries Visit Malaysia the Most? Tourist arrivals per million population in log scale from 2020 to 2024 (hover a country for details)
Long-Term Arrival Trends Annual arrivals into Malaysia from 1995 to 2024
  • Malaysia's inbound tourism grew steadily from 7.5 million in 1996 to a peak of 27.4 million in 2015.
  • The 2020 border closure caused the sharpest collapse on record — arrivals plummeted to 470,559 in 2021.
  • Recovery has been swift, with 31.9 million arrivals in 2024 surpassing the pre-pandemic baseline.
Quarterly Arrivals by Top 5 Countries Quarterly arrivals from 2020 to 2024
  • Singapore consistently leads arrivals, driven by frequent cross-border travel.
  • China shows strong seasonal peaks post-2022, while Indonesia and Thailand maintain steady volumes.
  • Brunei's arrivals remain modest but stable.

Where Do They Land?

Arrivals by Point of Entry Total tourist arrivals by point of entry from 2020 to 2023 scaled by volume (hover a point for details)
Top 10 Entry Points by Total Arrivals Total arrivals at each entry point from 2020 to 2023
  • Bangunan Sultan Iskandar recorded over 17 million arrivals — more than double the next busiest point.
  • KLIA Terminals 1 and 2 lead among air entry points.
  • Stulang Laut is the only sea entry point in the top 10.
Arrivals by Entry Type Over Time Quarterly arrivals by entry type from 2020 to 2023
  • Land entry dominated, accounting for 66.8% of total arrivals between 2020 and 2023.
  • Air travel collapsed during 2020–2021 but recovered strongly from mid-2022.
  • Sea arrivals remain the smallest share at just 4.5% throughout the period.
03

The Crash & The Comeback

Year-on-Year Change in Tourist Arrivals Annual percentage change in tourist arrivals from 2016 to 2024
  • Modest growth from 2016 to 2019 was followed by a collapse of 76.4% in 2020 and 92.4% in 2021.
  • The 2022 border reopening triggered a historic rebound of 3,119%.
  • Growth stabilised at 101.4% in 2023 and 4.5% in 2024.
Domestic Expenditure Recovery Domestic tourism expenditure in RM million from 2017 to 2024
  • Domestic tourism expenditure peaked at RM103 billion in 2019 before crashing to RM18 billion in 2021.
  • By 2024, expenditure reached RM107 billion, surpassing the pre-pandemic high.
  • The recovery signals a full resurgence in travel spending.
Country Arrival Recovery: 2022 vs 2024 Comparing tourist arrivals of the top 8 source countries between 2022 and 2024
  • 2022 marks the first full year of border reopening, making it the ideal baseline to measure post-pandemic recovery.
  • Singapore remains the dominant source market, reaching 15 million arrivals in 2024.
  • China recorded the steepest recovery, growing nearly 10x from 294,311 in 2022 to 3.2 million in 2024.
  • Click a country in the legend to isolate its trend.
Domestic Visitor Recovery Annual domestic visitors into Malaysia from 2017 to 2024
  • Domestic visitors collapsed from 92.6 million in 2019 to just 17.5 million in 2021.
  • Visitors rebounded to 98.4 million by 2024.
  • The stepped recovery confirms that confidence in domestic travel returned quickly once restrictions lifted.
04

A Closer Look

Monthly Tourist Arrivals by Year Monthly arrival intensity from 2020 to 2024
  • The pandemic void of 2020–2021 is starkly visible as near-empty months across the grid.
  • Arrivals surged from April 2022 following the full reopening of borders.
  • By 2023–2024, monthly arrivals consistently exceeded 2.5 million, signalling a full recovery.
Tourist Arrivals by Gender Annual male and female inbound arrivals from 2020 to 2024
  • Male arrivals have consistently outnumbered female arrivals across all years.
  • The gap was narrowest in 2021 and widest in 2023 at 3.4 million.
  • By 2024, female arrivals reached 14.7 million vs. 17.1 million male, suggesting a shift toward balance.
