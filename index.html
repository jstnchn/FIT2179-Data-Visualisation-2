Global Context

"Singapore, Indonesia and China consistently top the list of visitor origins."

Malaysia's geographic position at the heart of Southeast Asia makes it naturally accessible to its neighbours. ASEAN countries account for the majority of arrivals.

Entry Points

"KLIA and the Johor-Singapore land crossings dominate Malaysia's entry points."

Malaysia has 84 official points of entry — by air, land, and sea. The Johor Bahru land crossing with Singapore alone accounts for a significant share of all arrivals.

84 official points of entry in Malaysia
Land dominant mode of entry — over 50% of arrivals
